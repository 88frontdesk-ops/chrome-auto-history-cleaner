Privacy Policy — History Auto Cleaner

Effective date: September 19, 2026

History Auto Cleaner is a Chrome extension that helps users automatically delete Chrome browsing history according to settings chosen by the user.

Information we access

The extension uses Chrome's history permission solely to delete browsing history when scheduled cleanup runs or when the user initiates cleanup. It does not read, analyze, record, or transmit websites, URLs, page titles, or other contents of browsing history.

Information we collect

History Auto Cleaner does not collect personal information, browsing history, visited URLs, website content, search queries, or other browsing activity.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Local storage

User preferences, such as cleanup intervals and enabled settings, are stored locally using Chrome's storage facilities and are not transmitted to a server by the extension.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published in the project repository.

Contact

For questions or concerns, please open an issue in the project repository.
