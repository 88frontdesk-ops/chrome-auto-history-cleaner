# Privacy Policy — History Auto Cleaner

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

History Auto Cleaner is a Chrome extension that helps users automatically delete their Chrome browsing history according to settings chosen by the user.

## Information we access

The extension uses Chrome's `history` permission solely to delete browsing history when:

- A scheduled cleanup runs;
- The user selects the manual cleanup action; or
- The user enables the optional cleanup-on-close feature, subject to Chrome's extension lifecycle limitations.

The extension does not read, analyze, record, or transmit the websites, URLs, page titles, or other contents of the user's browsing history.

## Information we collect

History Auto Cleaner does **not** collect personal information, browsing history, visited URLs, website content, search queries, or other browsing activity.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

## Local storage

The extension stores user preferences, such as the selected cleanup interval and enabled settings, locally using Chrome's storage facilities. These settings are not transmitted to a server by the extension.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

## Data security

Because the extension does not transmit browsing data or personal information to a remote server, it does not maintain a remote database of user activity.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published in this repository.
